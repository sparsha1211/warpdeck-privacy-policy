WarpDeck Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026 · Effective: November 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Bearcat Inc (“Bearcat Inc,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information in connection with the WarpDeck mobile / VR application (the “App”).

Developer: Bearcat Inc
Publisher: Bearcat Inc
Application: WarpDeck (com.warpdeck.launcher)

WarpDeck is a boot quick-launch utility for Meta Quest / Horizon OS and other Android-based VR headsets, developed and published by Bearcat Inc.

1. Data We Collect

None. Bearcat Inc does not collect, receive, transmit, sell, or share any personal data through WarpDeck.

  • WarpDeck does not request the Android INTERNET permission and has no network access.
  • WarpDeck contains no analytics, advertising, tracking, or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • WarpDeck requires no account, sign-in, or registration.
  • Bearcat Inc operates no servers that receive data from WarpDeck.

2. Data Stored Only on Your Device

To function, WarpDeck stores the following preferences locally in the App’s private on-device storage. This data never leaves your device and is never transmitted to Bearcat Inc or any third party:

  • Your chosen boot / auto-launch app and Quick Launch (Top 5) apps, including their package and component names and order;
  • Boot delay, countdown timer, boot mode, and launch-on-boot settings;
  • Panel preferences (preferred refresh rate, keep-awake) and optional performance-profile choices (refresh rate, CPU/GPU levels, render resolution, foveation, and apply-on-boot preference).

WarpDeck also reads, locally and only at runtime:

  • Installed-app names, package names, and icons via the Android package manager, using manifest <queries> declarations (MAIN/LAUNCHER, LEANBACK_LAUNCHER, and VR_HOME) — solely to display your app list;
  • Device status shown in the App (display refresh rate and modes, battery level and charging state, thermal state, and current debug.oculus.* system-property values) — solely to display live headset status to you.

None of the above is collected by, transmitted to, or stored by Bearcat Inc.

3. Permissions WarpDeck Requests

  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED — to show the boot countdown / Quick Launch panel after the headset restarts, if you enable launch-on-boot;
  • SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM — for precise boot-delay timing (WarpDeck works with approximate timing if this is not granted);
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS — for the fallback full-screen notification if a direct countdown start is blocked;
  • USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT — for that same fallback notification.

WarpDeck does not request INTERNET, QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES, location, camera, microphone, contacts, storage, or any other sensitive permission.

4. How Data Is Used

On-device preferences and runtime reads are used solely to provide WarpDeck’s features: displaying your app list, showing the boot countdown, launching your chosen app, and displaying headset status. There is no advertising, profiling, analytics, or cross-app tracking.

5. Data Sharing

Bearcat Inc does not share any data with third parties, because WarpDeck transmits no data to Bearcat Inc or to anyone else.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Preferences remain only on your device until you delete them. You may delete all WarpDeck data at any time by either:

  • Uninstalling WarpDeck; or
  • Going to system Settings → Apps → WarpDeck → Storage → Clear storage / Clear data.

Deletion is immediate and permanent. Because nothing is transmitted to us, there is no server copy for Bearcat Inc to delete.

7. Security

WarpDeck preferences are kept in the App’s private, sandboxed on-device storage protected by the operating system. Since WarpDeck has no network access and transmits nothing, there is no data in transit to intercept.

8. Children’s Privacy

WarpDeck does not collect personal data from anyone, including children, and does not knowingly collect information from children under 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction).

9. Changes to This Policy

Bearcat Inc may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted at this URL with a revised “Last updated” date. Continued use of WarpDeck after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
