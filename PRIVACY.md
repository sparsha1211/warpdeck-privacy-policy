# WarpDeck Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 19, 2026. Effective: November 19, 2026._

**Developer: Bearcat Inc**
**Publisher: Bearcat Inc**
**Application: WarpDeck (`com.warpdeck.launcher`)**

This Privacy Policy describes how Bearcat Inc ("Bearcat Inc," "we," "us," or
"our") handles information in connection with WarpDeck, a boot quick-launch
utility for Meta Quest / Horizon OS and other Android-based VR headsets,
developed and published by Bearcat Inc.

## 1. Data we collect

**None.** Bearcat Inc does not collect, receive, transmit, sell, or share any
personal data through WarpDeck.

- WarpDeck does not request the Android `INTERNET` permission and has no
  network access.
- WarpDeck contains no analytics, advertising, tracking, or crash-reporting SDKs.
- WarpDeck requires no account, sign-in, or registration.
- Bearcat Inc operates no servers that receive data from WarpDeck.

## 2. Data stored only on your device

To function, WarpDeck stores these preferences locally in the app's private
on-device storage. This data never leaves your device and is never transmitted
to Bearcat Inc or any third party:

- Your chosen boot / auto-launch app and Quick Launch (Top 5) apps, including
  their package and component names and order;
- Boot delay, countdown timer, boot mode, and launch-on-boot settings;
- Panel preferences (preferred refresh rate, keep-awake) and optional
  performance-profile choices (refresh rate, CPU/GPU levels, render
  resolution, foveation, and apply-on-boot preference).

WarpDeck also reads, locally and only at runtime:

- Installed-app names, package names, and icons via the Android package
  manager, using manifest `<queries>` declarations (MAIN/LAUNCHER,
  LEANBACK_LAUNCHER, and VR_HOME) — solely to display your app list;
- Device status shown in the app (display refresh rate and modes, battery
  level and charging state, thermal state, and current `debug.oculus.*`
  system-property values) — solely to display live headset status to you.

None of the above is collected by, transmitted to, or stored by Bearcat Inc.

## 3. Permissions WarpDeck requests

- `RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED` — to show the boot countdown / Quick Launch panel
  after the headset restarts, if you enable launch-on-boot;
- `SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM` — for precise boot-delay timing (WarpDeck works with
  approximate timing if this is not granted);
- `POST_NOTIFICATIONS` — for the fallback full-screen notification if a
  direct countdown start is blocked;
- `USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT` — for that same fallback notification.

WarpDeck does not request `INTERNET`, `QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES`, location, camera,
microphone, contacts, storage, or any other sensitive permission.

## 4. How data is used

On-device preferences and runtime reads are used solely to provide WarpDeck's
features: displaying your app list, showing the boot countdown, launching your
chosen app, and displaying headset status. There is no advertising, profiling,
analytics, or cross-app tracking.

## 5. Data sharing

Bearcat Inc does not share any data with third parties, because WarpDeck
transmits no data to Bearcat Inc or to anyone else.

## 6. Data retention and deletion

Preferences remain only on your device until you delete them. Delete all
WarpDeck data at any time by uninstalling WarpDeck, or via system Settings →
Apps → WarpDeck → Storage → Clear storage / Clear data. Deletion is immediate
and permanent. Because nothing is transmitted to us, there is no server copy
for Bearcat Inc to delete.

## 7. Security

Preferences are kept in the app's private, sandboxed on-device storage
protected by the operating system. Since WarpDeck has no network access and
transmits nothing, there is no data in transit to intercept.

## 8. Children's privacy

WarpDeck does not collect personal data from anyone, including children, and
does not knowingly collect information from children under 13 (or the
applicable age in your jurisdiction).

posted at the public Privacy Policy URL with a revised "Last updated" date.
